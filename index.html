What counts as a change?

Every monitored source is downloaded and compared against its previous text fingerprint. A difference creates a dated Changed alert; an inaccessible source creates an Attention alert instead.

The watcher does not automatically rewrite exam or deadline facts. Those must be confirmed from official 2027/28 rules first.
